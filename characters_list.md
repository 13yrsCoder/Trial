# THE SHADOW ARCHIVE
## Character List

### MAJOR CHARACTERS

**ALEX RIVERS (28) - Protagonist**
- **Role:** Reincarnated Shadow Archivist, Librarian
- **Personality:** Intelligent, curious, morally conflicted, determined
- **Power:** Memory Reader → Shadow Walker → Shadow Archivist
- **Motivation:** Recover his lost memories and understand his true identity
- **Secret:** His current life is a constructed reality; he committed genocide in his past life
- **Faction:** Independent (formerly Archive Guardian)

**SARAH CHEN (26) - Deuteragonist**
- **Role:** Memory Hunter, Alex's ally
- **Personality:** Cautious, loyal, haunted by her own lost memories
- **Power:** Former Archive Guardian, skilled in memory manipulation
- **Motivation:** Recover her own lost memories and protect Alex
- **Secret:** She was once Alex's apprentice in his previous life
- **Faction:** Memory Hunters (formerly Archive Council)

**EMMA RIVERS (25) - Antagonist**
- **Role:** Alex's "sister," Memory Hunter
- **Personality:** Manipulative, ambitious, ruthless when necessary
- **Power:** Memory Weaver, skilled in deception and memory theft
- **Motivation:** Find the Archive Key to gain ultimate power
- **Secret:** She's not Alex's real sister; she's been using him from the beginning
- **Faction:** Memory Hunters

**THE LIBRARIAN (Unknown Age) - Mysterious Ally**
- **Role:** Mysterious figure who helps Alex escape the Archive Council
- **Personality:** Wise, cryptic, seems to know more than he reveals
- **Power:** Unknown, but clearly powerful
- **Motivation:** Protect the Archive and guide Alex
- **Secret:** He's actually a fragment of Alex's previous consciousness
- **Faction:** Independent

### ARCHIVE COUNCIL

**COUNCILOR MARCUS WELLS (45) - Primary Antagonist**
- **Role:** High-ranking member of the Archive Council
- **Personality:** Cold, calculating, believes the ends justify the means
- **Power:** Archive Guardian, expert in memory erasure
- **Motivation:** Destroy the Archive to prevent its misuse
- **Secret:** He was present during Alex's genocide and helped cover it up
- **Faction:** Archive Council

**COUNCILOR ELENA SANTOS (38) - Secondary Antagonist**
- **Role:** Archive Council member, Marcus's right hand
- **Personality:** Ruthless, efficient, shows no mercy to threats
- **Power:** Memory Weaver, specializes in torture and interrogation
- **Motivation:** Eliminate all threats to the Council's control
- **Secret:** She's actually a Memory Eater in human form
- **Faction:** Archive Council

**COUNCILOR JAMES BLACKWOOD (52) - Moderate Antagonist**
- **Role:** Archive Council member, more moderate than others
- **Personality:** Pragmatic, willing to negotiate, but still dangerous
- **Power:** Shadow Walker, expert in shadow realm navigation
- **Motivation:** Maintain balance between factions
- **Secret:** He secretly sympathizes with Alex but can't act openly
- **Faction:** Archive Council

### MEMORY HUNTERS

**DAMON KINCAID (32) - Rival/Ally**
- **Role:** Leader of a Memory Hunter faction
- **Personality:** Charismatic, opportunistic, but has a code of honor
- **Power:** Memory Weaver, expert in memory trading
- **Motivation:** Profit and power, but also survival
- **Secret:** He knows the truth about the Archive's sentience
- **Faction:** Memory Hunters

**LILY ZHANG (29) - Ally**
- **Role:** Memory Hunter, Sarah's friend
- **Personality:** Loyal, brave, willing to risk everything for friends
- **Power:** Memory Reader, skilled in information gathering
- **Motivation:** Help Sarah and Alex, protect the innocent
- **Secret:** She's actually a former Archive Council member who defected
- **Faction:** Memory Hunters

**CARTER REED (35) - Antagonist**
- **Role:** Ruthless Memory Hunter
- **Personality:** Sadistic, enjoys causing pain, completely amoral
- **Power:** Shadow Binding, specializes in trapping people in nightmares
- **Motivation:** Cause suffering and gain power
- **Secret:** He's working for the Shadow Court
- **Faction:** Memory Hunters (secretly Shadow Court)

### SHADOW COURT

**THE VOID KING (Unknown Age) - Major Antagonist**
- **Role:** Ruler of the Shadow Court
- **Personality:** Ancient, alien, incomprehensible to humans
- **Power:** Reality Weaving, can rewrite aspects of reality
- **Motivation:** Consume the Archive's power to become omnipotent
- **Secret:** He's actually a fragment of the original Shadow Archivist
- **Faction:** Shadow Court

**THE MEMORY QUEEN (Unknown Age) - Manipulator**
- **Role:** Second-in-command of the Shadow Court
- **Personality:** Seductive, manipulative, enjoys playing with emotions
- **Power:** Nightmare Projection, can force others to experience their worst fears
- **Motivation:** Serve the Void King and gain power
- **Secret:** She's in love with Alex's previous incarnation
- **Faction:** Shadow Court

**THE SHADOW PRINCE (Unknown Age) - Wild Card**
- **Role:** Enigmatic member of the Shadow Court
- **Personality:** Unpredictable, seems to help and hinder randomly
- **Power:** Archive Theft, can steal memories from anyone
- **Motivation:** Unknown, seems to have his own agenda
- **Secret:** He's actually trying to save the Archive from the Void King
- **Faction:** Shadow Court (secretly independent)

### HOLLOW ONES

**THOMAS GRAY (40) - Tragic Figure**
- **Role:** Leader of the Hollow Ones
- **Personality:** Empty, desperate, willing to do anything for memories
- **Power:** None, but has knowledge of memory magic
- **Motivation:** Recover his lost memories at any cost
- **Secret:** He was once a powerful Memory Weaver before losing his memories
- **Faction:** Hollow Ones

**MARY WHITE (35) - Victim**
- **Role:** Hollow One, Thomas's follower
- **Personality:** Fragile, easily manipulated, desperate for hope
- **Power:** None
- **Motivation:** Find someone who can restore her memories
- **Secret:** She's actually a spy for the Archive Council
- **Faction:** Hollow Ones (secretly Archive Council)
